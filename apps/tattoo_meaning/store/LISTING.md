## Description (iOS + Play)

Photograph a tattoo and read what the ink often means. Tattoo Meaning looks at the photo and returns a short cultural reading: the motif name, a plain-language summary, likely origin, and related symbols.

Use the camera or pick a photo from your library. Save the ones you want to keep. History holds every scan. Collection holds the ones you star.

Three reads are free. Tattoo Meaning Pro unlocks unlimited reads, full history, and your personal collection. Subscribe weekly with a free trial, or yearly. Restore purchases anytime. Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google.

Results are cultural interpretations, not medical, legal, fortune-telling, or a claim about the person wearing the tattoo.
