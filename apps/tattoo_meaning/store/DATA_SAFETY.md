# Play Data safety / Apple privacy

Match the binary. No accounts.

| Category | Collect | Linked to identity | Used for tracking |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Photos | Yes, user-chosen tattoo photos | No | No |
| Purchases | Yes, StoreKit / Play Billing | No | No |
| Crash logs | Store diagnostics only | No | No |

Photos are sent to Google Gemini only when a live read is compiled in with a Gemini key. Otherwise the on-device guide is used and labeled as not a custom photo read.

No location, no contacts, no advertising ID, no analytics SDK.
