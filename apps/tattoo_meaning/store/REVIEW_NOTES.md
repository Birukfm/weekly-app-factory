# App Review notes

Paste into App Store Connect and Play Console.

## What to test

1. Complete onboarding (3 steps) and skip or buy the paywall.
2. Scan tab → Camera or Photo library → pick a tattoo photo.
3. A meaning screen appears. Save to collection. Check History.
4. Three free reads, then the paywall returns.
5. Settings → Restore purchases.

Sandbox IAP product IDs: `tattoomeaning_weekly`, `tattoomeaning_yearly`.

If live Gemini is not compiled in, the app still shows an on-device symbolism guide and labels it as not a custom photo read.

## Account

No login. No demo account.

## Notes

The app photographs tattoos to explain cultural symbolism. It does not identify people, gangs, or medical conditions.
