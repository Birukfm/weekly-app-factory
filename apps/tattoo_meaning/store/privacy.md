# Privacy policy

**App name:** Tattoo Meaning  
**Contact:** Replace with your support email before submit.

## Data we collect

- Photos you take or pick so the app can explain a tattoo's meaning. Images are sent to the identification API (Google Gemini or similar) when you run a scan. We do not run a public gallery of your photos.
- Purchase status from Apple App Store or Google Play.
- Crash-free diagnostics provided by the store.

We do not create user accounts in this version.

## Third parties

- Apple and Google for in-app purchases.
- Google Gemini for image interpretation when a live read is available.

## Your choices

- Restore purchases from Settings.
- Skip Pro and use the limited free scans.
- Email support to ask questions about data.

## Changes

We will update this page if data practices change.
